Typing Through History

1868

First Typewriter Patent

Christopher Latham Sholes, Carlos Glidden, and Samuel W. Soule were granted a patent for the first practical typewriter.

1873

QWERTY Layout Introduced

The QWERTY keyboard layout was designed to prevent mechanical jams by separating commonly used letter pairs.

1936

IBM Electric Typewriter

IBM released its first electric typewriter, making typing easier and less physically demanding.

1946

First Computer Keyboard

The ENIAC computer introduced keyboard input, beginning the transition from typewriters to computer keyboards.

1981

IBM PC Keyboard

The IBM Model F keyboard standardized the layout still used in modern keyboards.

2010s

Mechanical Keyboard Revival

Enthusiasts rediscovered the benefits of mechanical keyboards, leading to a renaissance in keyboard technology and customization.
