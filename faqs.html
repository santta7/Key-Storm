Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is KeyStorm?

KeyStorm is a typing practice platform designed to improve your typing speed and accuracy.

2. How can I track my progress?

You can track your progress through the stats section in your profile.

3. Is there a mobile version of KeyStorm?

Yes, KeyStorm is accessible on mobile devices through your web browser.

4. Can I compete with others?

Yes, you can participate in ranked typing tests and compete on leaderboards.

5. How do I contact support?

You can contact support through the contact page or email us directly at support@keystorm.com.
